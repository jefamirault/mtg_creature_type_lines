Creature Type-Line Groups ✦

Every pair of cards in a group shares a creature subtype — but no subtype is common to the whole group. All type lines within a group are unique. Vintage-legal, single-faced cards only. Which card fills a slot is arbitrary: use a card’s ▲▼ arrows to cycle through every other card carrying that slot’s subtypes — including cards that add more, shown as dimmed chips.
Pattern A — 4 creatures with at least 6 subtypes that each appear on two or more of them.
Pattern B — 5 creatures over exactly 5 subtypes, arranged 123 / 124 / 345 / 15 / 25 (each digit a subtype, each row one creature).
Pattern C — Pattern A or B groups led by two Commander Legends partner commanders, at least one white; the rest of the group fits within the commanders' combined color identity.
